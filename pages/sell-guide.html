The 5 steps

1

Create your free account

Sign up with your email (or Google) — takes 30 seconds, no shop registration needed. Create an account →

2

Tap "Sell" and pick a category

Phones & Electronics, Fashion, Cars, Property, or Services — pick whichever fits your item.

3

Add a clear title, price and location

Be specific — "iPhone 13 Pro · 256GB · Clean" sells faster than "Phone for sale." Add your price in USD; buyers see the SSP value automatically.

4

Add real photos

Listings with clear, real photos get up to 5× more interest. Take them in good light, show the item from a few angles — no stock photos.

5

Publish and reply fast

Your listing goes live instantly. Buyers message you right in the app — reply quickly, sellers who respond fast sell faster.

Questions sellers ask

Is it really free to list?

Yes — posting is completely free, no limit on how many items. Paid boosts are optional, only if you want extra visibility.

How do buyers pay me?

You and the buyer arrange payment and pickup/delivery directly — Nile Link is currently a listings and messaging platform. In-app payments and escrow are coming in a future update.

What if I want to edit or remove a listing?

Go to your Dashboard any time — you can edit the price or title, mark an item as sold, or delete it, in one tap.

How do I get the verified seller badge?

Verification is being rolled out to active sellers — message us via Help & Support and we'll get you set up.
