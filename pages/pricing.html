Before you decide

Pricing questions, answered

Is the free plan really free?
Yes — completely. Listing is always free, with unlimited listings, in-app messaging and photos. It's a permanent plan, not a trial: no card required, no auto-charges, and it never converts to a paid plan on its own.
How do I pay for boosts and plans?
Pay via mobile money (mPesa, Zain Cash), bank transfer or card. All prices are in USD with live SSP equivalents shown, so you always know today's value in South Sudanese Pounds.
Does a boost guarantee my item will sell?
No — and we won't pretend otherwise. A boost puts your listing in front of more buyers (top of category results, or the homepage). Whether it sells still depends on your photos, price and how quickly you reply. Boosts amplify a good listing; they can't fix a weak one.
What's the difference between plan boosts and one-off boosts?
Plans bundle boosts monthly — Plus includes 2 free boosts a month, Pro Seller 5, and Business 15. One-off boosts are pay-as-you-go for a single listing (like the $2 seven-day boost), with no subscription needed. Casual sellers can stay on the free plan and buy a boost only when they want one.
Can I change or cancel a paid plan?
Plans are billed monthly (or yearly at 20% off, if you pick the yearly option). To upgrade, downgrade or cancel — or if a payment went wrong — message our team via Help & Support and we'll sort it out. Your listings stay live on the free plan either way.
Why are prices in USD and SSP?
Prices are set in USD to stay stable, and we show live SSP equivalents next to every plan so you always see what it costs in South Sudanese Pounds at today's rate.
