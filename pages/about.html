Why we exist

One trusted place to trade

We started Nile Link with one belief: people here deserve a marketplace as good as anywhere in the world — one that understands local prices, local cities, and local trust.

Every day, thousands of South Sudanese buy and sell through their phones. But too often deals happen in scattered groups, with no way to know who's real. Nile Link brings it all into one trusted place: verified sellers, secure messaging, and prices in both USD and SSP with a live exchange rate.
