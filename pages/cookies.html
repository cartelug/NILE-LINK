Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

This policy explains how Nile Link uses cookies and similar browser storage. The short version: we only use what's needed to make the site work and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

1. What are cookies & local storage?

Cookies and "local storage" are small pieces of data a website saves in your browser. They let a site remember things between visits — like keeping you signed in so you don't have to log in on every page. Local storage stays on your device and is not sent to advertisers.

2. What we use — and why

Nile Link uses a small number of essential storage items. These are required for the site to function, so they are always on. We do not set analytics or advertising cookies.

3. Third-party services

A few trusted services we use may set their own storage in order to work:

  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google's own cookies and privacy policy apply to that step.
  • Google Fonts — used to display the site's typeface.
  • Supabase — our authentication and database provider, which stores the secure sign-in token described above.

We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any cross-site tracking.

4. Managing your choices

When you first visit, we show a short notice so you can Accept or Decline non-essential storage. Because our preference items only make the site more convenient for you (and never track you across other sites), declining will not break core browsing. You can also clear or block cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings — though signing out and clearing essential items will log you out and reset your preferences.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. When we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date above.
