Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Your trust matters. This policy explains what information Nile Link ("we", "us", "our") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Nile Link website and marketplace.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us: when you create an account we collect your name, email address and, if you set one, a password (stored securely in hashed form — we never see your plain password). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. When you use the marketplace we collect the city you select, the listings and photos you post, the messages you send, offers you make, and reports you file.

Information collected automatically: basic technical data needed to run the site, such as your device and browser type and approximate location by city. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

We only collect what we need to run the marketplace.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to create and operate your account, show your listings to buyers, enable messaging between users, send you service notifications (such as new messages and requests), display prices in USD with a live SSP estimate from a public exchange-rate source, prevent fraud and abuse, respond to reports and support requests, and improve the Service. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Legal bases

Where applicable law requires it, we process your information to perform our contract with you (running your account and the marketplace), for our legitimate interests (keeping the platform safe and improving it), to comply with legal obligations, and — where relevant — with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Service providers we use

We rely on trusted third parties to run Nile Link, and share only the data needed for them to provide their service:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication and file storage provider, which hosts your account data, listings, photos and messages.
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google" for sign-in; Google's own privacy policy applies to that step.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website files and serves the site.
  • Google Fonts — serves the site's typeface.

These providers process data on our behalf and may store it on servers outside South Sudan. We take reasonable steps to ensure your information is protected wherever it is processed.

5. What other users can see

Your public profile (such as your display name and city) and your active listings are visible to other users. Your email address is never shown to other users automatically. Messages are shared only with the person you are chatting with. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

6. Messaging

Messages between buyers and sellers are stored so your conversations stay available to you. Please keep sensitive personal or payment details out of chat and follow our safety tips.

7. Cookies & local storage

We use a small amount of essential browser storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences such as your language and saved listings. We do not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies. For the full list of what we store and how to manage it, see our Cookie Policy.

8. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your listings and personal profile data from public view and delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we must keep some records to comply with the law or resolve disputes.

9. Your rights & choices

You can edit your profile, change your language, manage notifications, or delete your account at any time from Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or restrict the use of your personal data. To make a request, contact us at privacy@nilelink.ss.

10. Children

Nile Link is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure password storage. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please protect your account and report anything suspicious.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email privacy@nilelink.ss or visit our Help Center.

See also our Terms of Use and Cookie Policy.
