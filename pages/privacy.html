Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Your trust matters. This policy explains what information Nile Link collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

We collect the phone number you sign up with, your name and city, the listings and messages you create, and basic device and usage data (such as pages viewed and approximate location by city). We do not collect more than we need to run the marketplace.

2. How we use it

We use your information to operate your account, show your listings to buyers, enable messaging, send you relevant notifications, prevent fraud, and improve the product. Prices are shown in USD with a live SSP estimate using a public exchange-rate source.

3. What we share

Your public profile and listings are visible to other users. Your phone number is never shared automatically — it is only revealed when you choose to share it in chat or enable the reveal-number feature. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

4. Messaging

Messages between buyers and sellers are stored so conversations stay available to you. Keep sensitive personal or payment details out of chat and follow our safety tips.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your language, saved listings, recently viewed items and sign-in session. These stay on your device and are not used for cross-site tracking.

6. Your choices

You can edit your profile, manage notification preferences, change your language, or delete your account at any time from Settings. Deleting your account removes your listings and personal data from public view.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including phone-based verification. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, so please protect your account and report anything suspicious.

8. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email privacy@nilelink.ss or visit our Help Center.
