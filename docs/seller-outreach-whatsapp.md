Ready to copy-paste for Rico's network (Juba sellers + diaspora contacts).
Per the Master Hub launch plan (Week 3–4: "Rico onboards first 10–15 real
sellers"). Keep it personal — a name at the top always beats a broadcast.

---

```

services). We're onboarding our first sellers in Juba this month.

photo, price and location, and buyers message you right in the app.

👉 https://cartelug.github.io/NILE-LINK/pages/sell-guide.html

If you post this month you also get free priority placement — just
reply here once you've signed up and I'll set it up for you.

Any questions, just ask!
```

## Tips for Rico when sending these

- Send **1-to-1** to your first 10–15 contacts before posting in any group — personal messages convert far better than broadcasts.
- If someone replies interested but hesitant, offer to **sit with them for their first listing** (2 minutes on a call/in person) — this is the single biggest lever for early conversion.
- Track who you've contacted and whether they posted in a simple list/sheet — this becomes your "first sellers" traction number for the funding deck (per the Master Hub funding ask).
- Once someone's signed up, you (Neeza) can verify them from the [Admin panel](https://cartelug.github.io/NILE-LINK/pages/admin.html) → Sellers tab → Verify — do this for the first cohort so their listings carry the trust badge.
